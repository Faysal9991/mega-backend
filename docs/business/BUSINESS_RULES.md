# Approved identity rules — Step 8

Source: owner Step 8 request; no legacy authentication behavior is an implementation requirement. Concrete contracts/defaults are in ../architecture/IDENTITY_AUTH.md.

- IDN-001: only nonce-bound, server-verified Google login creates an ordinary account. Provider google + verified sub are authoritative. Returning login never overwrites ANY profile/contact field; no email/phone linking, phone signup, email/password login, OTP or public password signup.
- IDN-002: username/phone aliases are unique and set only with authenticated, fresh Google identifiers proof. Canonical phones are UNVERIFIED aliases, not ownership or recovery evidence. Conflicts never transfer aliases.
- IDN-003: initial password and recovery require fresh single-use proof from the linked Google subject; changes require that proof or current password plus live session. All sessions are atomically revoked after password mutation. Argon2id and durable pre-work budgets are mandatory.
- IDN-004: opaque hashed access/refresh secrets; single-use refresh rotation; reuse revokes its family. Clients serialize refresh and reauthenticate after ambiguous failure. Session/account/installation state is PostgreSQL-authoritative on every protected request. Redis absence cannot weaken security.
- IDN-005: installation IDs bind requests/sessions and restrictions, but are resettable, not physical-device attestation. Restricted accounts retain their Google binding and cannot sign up again through the same subject; restricted installations cannot create another account. Reset-resistant physical bans require a separately approved attestation design.

Defaults (not new business roles): 15-minute access, 30-day absolute family, 5 live families/account, 5-minute nonce/proof, exact normalization, rate and hashing budgets documented in IDENTITY_AUTH.md. No staff grants/business classification is inferred from Google/client input. Flexible permissions, agency/host workflows, broader profiles and push-token delivery remain deferred.

## Step 9 approved profile and scoped authorization rules

Self-edit only display_name/bio; null/unknown/credential/role/status/alias fields are rejected. Empty string clears, omission preserves; no normalization; concurrent changes to the same field use last commit. Name is at most 80 Unicode code points / 256 UTF-8 bytes, bio 500 code points / 2048 bytes; controls disallowed except LF/tab in bio. Existing Google seed data is retained without truncation. Identity keeps email, bindings, aliases, sessions and restrictions. Returning Google login never overwrites profiles.

Fixed permissions users.read, users.private.read (also users.read), users.profile.update and users.restrict are PostgreSQL-authoritative. Custom role names do not establish authority. Only separately protected owner manages staff roles/assignments; ordinary APIs cannot grant/change/remove protected owner. Bootstrap selects an existing active Google-linked account; no first-user promotion/account creation; one concurrent winner. Owners cannot be restricted or targeted by role assignment APIs; self-assignment is rejected. Restriction and session revocation commit together; lifting never revives sessions. All privileged mutations require atomic safe audit, including reason/correlation. Agency/host classification and premium entitlements remain separate and deferred. ## Owner-authorized diamond economy batch

One spendable diamond balance per account combines recharge, gift earnings, transfers and authorized commission/reward/adjustment sources while retaining immutable operation provenance. Current verified producers are transfers, configured gift distributions and explicitly audited protected-owner issuance/retirement; no unverified purchase/reward provider adapter is enabled. All money movement belongs to Wallet, uses checked whole-diamond int64 balanced postings, actor/operation-key fingerprints and atomic commit. Redis never authorizes spending or eligibility.

Commercial settings have no active defaults: transfer min/max/fees/caps, gift catalog/distribution/VAT and withdrawal review policy must be explicitly configured in immutable versions. Largest-remainder gift allocation and ceil transfer/withdrawal fee arithmetic are explicit supported policy choices; unsupported alternatives remain configuration-required. Inclusive diamond tax allocation is not fiat tax payment. Withdrawals are safe review-only reservations; approval is not payout, rejection/cancellation releases once, no request is settled without an integrated provider. Agency audio/video hosts additionally need current calendar month verified activity through eligibility check time: default at least 15 distinct qualifying days, each with at least 120 verified live minutes, configurable by immutable applicable audio/video/shared policy version. Qualification needs no month-end wait. The configured IANA business timezone defines day/month boundaries; completed authoritative intervals only, with overlaps deduplicated and no client duration or fabricated activity. Recheck atomically at withdrawal acceptance, independently of display. The next month resets the calculated count without modifying activity or withdrawal history. This owner-confirmed current-month rule supersedes the previous-completed-month proposal (ADR 0011); all other wallet/withdrawal requirements remain unchanged. See FINANCIAL_ENGINE.md / AGENCY_ACTIVITY.md for exact policy, timezone, interval, source and temporal-history semantics and remaining gates.

## LiveKit / agency authorized policy boundary

The owner-authorized batch at 645c75c is specified by ADR 0012 and LIVE_AGENCY.md. One immutable agency owner account ID identifies each agency and preserves financial references; one active agency/classification association per host. Approval closes no other association silently. Leaving/classification changes retain effective history. No universal user_type is introduced. Independent-host publishing and ownership transfer are unspecified and denied; cross-host-type compatibility defaults to deny until explicit configuration/immutable admin policy. Agency membership requests use host applications; agency ownership/managership alone confers no host classification or wallet authority. Audience may observe as authenticated active members; only eligible host/co-host/audio-seat participants qualify for activity. Moderator is a room role with no implicit publisher grant. Peers cannot moderate peer co-hosts/moderators; the host and scoped staff retain the stronger boundary.

Current calendar month, 15 distinct qualifying days and 120 verified minutes per day remain the approved initial withdrawal defaults, editable through existing immutable audio/video/shared activity policies with explicit IANA timezone. No month-end wait. Missing verified time is never guessed. New live policies/classifications cannot alter completed evidence or saved financial decisions. Historical transition proration remains unapproved; this batch preserves the existing calculator/Wallet policy selection without inventing proration or conversion rates. See consolidated evidence for real-provider and review gates.

Owner-confirmed lifecycle policy (ADR 0013): leaving clears the active agency/host eligibility context and preserves history. Joining another agency, or rejoining after leave, starts a new qualifying day/minute context from zero. Classification changes within the same uninterrupted agency retain its context. The append-only agency activity-context journal and association writes commit together through agency/postgres, also reused by the compatible financial-admin entry point. Existing non-agency Wallet rules apply without an active association; balances, ledgers and saved withdrawal decisions are unchanged. Independent financial review remains pending.

Account-restriction ceilings are append-only server timestamps. A completed interval that crosses a restriction is clipped at its first restriction; unrestriction cannot restore the discarded suffix. Fresh authenticated/verified connections may start later intervals. Completed activity before the restriction and historical financial decisions stay unchanged. Supported engineering limit: 50 active room memberships per account. Provider absence and empty-room presence differ; an unexpected provider SID never reopens a completed business session.

Owner clarification (ADR 0013): leaving clears current agency/host/eligibility context while retaining membership, verified activity and financial history. Existing Wallet current-association behavior is preserved. New-agency membership starts day/minute calculation from zero per the owner's follow-up; no balance/history erasure is authorized.

## Authorized catalog/media/push follow-up

ADR 0014 specifies live-gift catalog authority and explicit active_publishers recipient mode. Existing Wallet allocation, VAT, rounding, immutable financial policy, funds/role/idempotency rules remain unchanged. Live gifts additionally bind room/session and an immutable catalog version, committing a delivery intent atomically. Room event/provider acknowledgment is separate from financial commit and recipient receipt; duplicates never spend again. Non-room Wallet gifting retains its older policy catalog and emits no room animation. Presigned JPEG/PNG uploads are private until validated variants; avatar attachments require own approved assets and CAS. Ordinary accounts cannot upload agency/gift purposes. Banner is unsupported. No new commission/rate, fiat tax settlement, dynamic infrastructure or independent review approval is introduced.
